About

Shehan Dilhara is a Sri Lankan IT undergraduate and an aspiring Infrastructure and DevOps Engineer. As a technology enthusiast, he has a keen interest in Linux system administration, cloud technologies, software engineering, and artificial intelligence. He is deeply committed to continuous learning, hands-on experimentation, and the development of practical technical solutions, while actively expanding his expertise in IT infrastructure, automation, and emerging technologies.

Early life

Shehan was born in Badulla, Sri Lanka, and grew up in the Bibile area of the Monaragala District. His educational journey began at Mo/ Prof. Senaka Bibile Model School, where he completed the Grade 5 Scholarship Examination. He later attended Royal College, Monaragala, completing his Ordinary Level (O/L) examinations in 2020 and Advanced Level (A/L) examinations in the Commerce stream in 2023. After completing his A/Ls, he moved to Malabe to pursue higher education at the Sri Lanka Institute of Information Technology (SLIIT), where he is currently studying for a BSc (Hons) in Information Technology.
